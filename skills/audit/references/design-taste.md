# Design taste

The author's own design rules. `design-critique` reads this first: a rule here outranks `design-fundamentals.md` and any other general advice, and where a fundamental disagrees with a rule here, the fundamental isn't raised.

This file grows from real critiques. When the author turns down a suggestion with a reason, or states a preference while reviewing one, `design-critique` offers to add it here as a rule, in the author's words, and adds it only when they say yes. Nothing goes in on inference.

Each rule is one line under the heading it belongs to (add headings as they're needed), with the reason after it when the author gave one.
